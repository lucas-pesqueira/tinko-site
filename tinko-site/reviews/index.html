Can you remove bad reviews?

Only Google can remove reviews. I can report the ones that break Google's rules, and reply to the rest in a way that shows future customers you care.

Is it safe to give you access?

You add me as a Manager, not an owner. A Manager can't remove your profile, add or remove people, or take ownership, and you can remove me with one click. I only use access to reply to reviews.

Will you post anything without my OK?

During the first week, nothing goes live without your approval. After that, negative-review replies are always sent to you first.

Do you use AI?

I use AI tools to draft faster, but I review and edit every reply myself.

What if I want to stop?
